Five distinct catalog entries (0036–0040), based on three official manufacturer specification pages. Two Hokuyo H01 variants have distinct angular resolution and ambient-light limits compared with their non-H01 variants. All values are manufacturer-published, conditional where specified. Manufacturer product pages do not establish orderable SKU, procurement availability, current firmware, ROS2 interoperability, real-world performance, or safety suitability. Do not treat listed as hardware verified.
