# Wave 7 evidence review

Official manufacturer sources were reviewed for each model. Manufacturer claims are conditional and not independent test results.

- 0031: 30 m is an assured white Kent-sheet range under manufacturer conditions; IP67 applies to optics excluding Ethernet connector.
- 0032: 100 m is a maximum output limitation, NOT assured range; 30 m assured detection is subject to target conditions.
- 0033: A2M7 only; A2M8/A2M12 specifications must not be inherited.
- 0034: Range figures are at 100 klx and specified reflectivity; older Horizon manual v1.0.
- 0035: Manufacturer typical specifications; certification claims are not independently audited.

Procurement SKU, hardware revision, product lifecycle, and ROS2 performance remain unverified.
